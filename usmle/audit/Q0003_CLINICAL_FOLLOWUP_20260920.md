## Clinical, numeric and second-answer review

Age 13 is inside the cited normal range for girls and matches the official USMLE adolescence band. “Several months” is narrative timing, not a diagnostic threshold. No dose, laboratory cutoff, risk percentage, conversion or management recommendation exists. Breast development plus growth acceleration is a normal gonadarche context.

The revised options are all real endocrine mechanisms. A is adrenarche, a distinct process that does not activate pituitary gonadotropins. C is suppressive receptor downregulation from sustained GnRH-agonist signaling. D suppresses gonadotropin release in hyperprolactinemia. E is peripheral gonadotropin-independent estrogen production. None initiates the normal HPG-axis activation requested by the lead. B remains the sole defensible answer.
