## Scoped sources inspected on 24 September 2026

- V2_UREA: Vasopressin increases phosphorylation of Ser84 and Ser486 in Slc14a2 collecting duct urea transporters. V2-selective dDAVP increases UT-A1/UT-A3 phosphorylation. Thus urea handling is not independent of the vasopressin pathway. Experimental mechanistic evidence, not a human clinical guideline. Locator: Abstract, dDAVP experiments in rat IMCD. https://pure.johnshopkins.edu/en/publications/vasopressin-increases-phosphorylation-of-ser84-and-ser486-in-slc1
- WILSON: Symptoms & Causes of Wilson Disease. ATP7B variants impair copper excretion into bile, causing retention. Locator: What causes Wilson disease?. https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/liver-disease/wilson-disease/symptoms-causes
- AIH: Definition & Facts for Autoimmune Hepatitis. Immune attack on liver causes inflammation and injury; this is a real competing hepatic mechanism, unlike invented AAT-receptor destruction. Locator: What is autoimmune hepatitis?. https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/liver-disease/autoimmune-hepatitis/definition-facts
- AAT: Alpha-1 antitrypsin deficiency. Reduced AAT permits elastase injury to alveoli, while abnormal AAT accumulates in liver. ZZ increases risk. Does not alone verify every ER-polymerization/histology detail. Locator: Causes; Inheritance. https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/condition/alpha-1-antitrypsin-deficiency/
- DMT1: Hypochromic microcytic anemia with iron overload. DMT1 dysfunction impairs erythroblast iron utilization; microcytic anemia may coexist with hepatic iron overload. Locator: Causes, SLC11A2 and erythroblast endosomal iron transport. https://medlineplus.gov/genetics/condition/hypochromic-microcytic-anemia-with-iron-overload/
